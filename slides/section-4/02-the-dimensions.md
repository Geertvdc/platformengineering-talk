# Slide 02 — Three Dimensions of Real Complexity

**Type:** Framework slide — three tensions the platform must resolve  
**Design:** Light slide, three columns or three stacked rows with labels

---

## Key Message

Every platform decision lives at the intersection of three tensions: self-service vs. guardrails, consistency vs. flexibility, and velocity vs. control. The tools you choose determine which side of each tension you default to.

---

## Talking Points

"Three dimensions show up in every platform conversation I've had."

"*First: self-service versus guardrails.* Developers want to provision resources themselves, now, without tickets. But platform teams are responsible for security, cost, compliance. You can't just hand out keys to Azure. The platform has to say yes automatically — but only to the right kind of yes."

"*Second: consistency versus flexibility.* Golden paths work when teams are willing to follow them. But teams have different stacks, different requirements, different cloud maturity. If the platform is too rigid, people route around it. Too flexible, and you're back to the snowflake problem."

"*Third: velocity versus control.* As AI makes it faster than ever to generate infrastructure code, the gap between 'developer wanting a resource' and 'resource appearing in production' is shrinking to seconds. Platforms built for weekly ticket cycles will not survive that velocity. But you still need audit trails, policy enforcement, and the ability to say 'not in that region.'"

"Every tool we're going to look at makes a different trade-off across these three dimensions. That's the lens."
