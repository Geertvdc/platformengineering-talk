# Slide 01 — Real-World Complexity

**Type:** Section pivot — grounding the audience in the messy reality  
**Design:** Dark slide, strong impact heading, list of real constraints

---

## Key Message

Theory is clean. Reality isn't. Every organisation has multiple teams, multiple accounts, existing Terraform, legacy managed services, and budget constraints. A good platform must work with all of that — not demand a clean-slate rewrite.

---

## Talking Points

"So we have the foundation: Kubernetes, GitOps, Argo CD. Declarative, self-healing, auditable. Great in theory."

"But here's the thing. You walk into a real organisation and it's never a clean slate. You've got five teams, each running in their own Azure subscription. You've got three years of Terraform modules the infrastructure team has built and debugged and trusts. You've got a compliance officer who needs to know exactly where every byte of data lives. You've got developers who have never touched a YAML file in their lives."

"And your budget meeting is next month."

"The platform can't just say 'throw everything away and do it our way.' That's not engineering — that's a rewrite project with no end date."

"So the real challenge of platform engineering isn't the happy path. It's: how do you bring consistency and self-service to an organisation that already exists, with all its history, all its constraints, and all its people?"

"That's what section four is about. We're going to get specific."
