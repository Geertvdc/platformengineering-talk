# Slide 04 — The Golden Path

**Type:** Core concept — opinionated self-service  
**Design:** Impact text around "GOLDEN PATH" or a single strong statement

---

## Key Message

The golden path is the platform's recommended way to do things — opinionated, but not a cage. It makes the right thing easy and the wrong thing intentional.

---

## Talking Points

"Platforms get a bad reputation for being rigid. 'You must do it our way or you get nothing.' That's not a platform — that's a control system. And developers will route around it the first chance they get."

"The right model is what Spotify popularised as the *golden path*: a recommended, well-maintained way to do the common things. Build a service, deploy it, connect it to a database — there's a path for that. It comes with all the guardrails, all the standards, all the integrations already built in."

"But it's not mandatory. If a team has a good reason to go off-path, they can. The platform doesn't stop them. It just doesn't hold their hand either."

"This is the key design principle: make the right thing easy, make the wrong thing possible but intentional. When someone goes off the golden path, that's a signal — either the path needs to be improved, or the team is doing something genuinely unusual."

"Most teams, most of the time, will take the golden path. Because it's easier. Because it's faster. Because someone else already solved the hard parts."

"This is how you get consistency without being a dictator."
