# Slide 01 — DevOps Grew Up

**Type:** Section pivot — platform engineering as DevOps evolved  
**Design:** Impact heading, dark slide, strong statement

---

## Key Message

Platform engineering is not the death of DevOps — it's what DevOps looks like when it matures at scale. "You build it, you run it" is the right principle. Platform engineering is the infrastructure that makes it actually work in a large organisation.

---

## Talking Points

"Before I tell you what platform engineering *is*, let me tell you what it isn't. It's not a rejection of DevOps. DevOps is not dead. The principles are sound."

"'You build it, you run it.' Ownership. Autonomy. No more throwing things over the wall to ops. This is still the goal. This is still right."

"But here's what nobody tells you in the DevOps books: 'you build it, you run it' works brilliantly for a team of eight. It starts to crack for a hundred engineers. And at a thousand engineers across thirty teams — you end up exactly where we started. Everyone running their own infrastructure, in their own way, with nobody to call when it breaks at 2am."

"The problem isn't the DevOps principle. The problem is that at scale, every team re-inventing the same infrastructure wheel is massively wasteful. And every team doing it slightly differently is a compliance nightmare."

"Platform engineering is the answer to that problem. It's 'you build it, you run it' — but with a shared foundation underneath that means you don't have to build *everything* from scratch. The platform handles the common parts. The team owns what's unique to their product."

"It's DevOps that scales."
