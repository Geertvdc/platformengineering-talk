# Slide 02 — Not an Ops Team

**Type:** Contrast — reframing the platform team role  
**Design:** Strong contrast slide, "NOT TICKETS." or similar

---

## Key Message

Platform engineering is a product discipline, not an ops function. The shift from ticket processor to product team changes everything.

---

## Talking Points

"Here's the trap a lot of organisations fall into: they take their ops team, rename them the 'platform team', and call it done. The same people, the same ticket queue, the same bottleneck — just with a new name on the door."

"That is not platform engineering."

"The difference is the *mindset*. An ops team is reactive — developers come to them with requests, they process them. A platform team is proactive — they build products that developers consume *without* ever having to ask."

"Think about it like this. When you use Stripe to add payments to your app, you don't email Stripe and wait for a ticket. You call an API. The Stripe team built a product that you self-serve from. That's the mental model."

"Your developers should be able to get a Kubernetes namespace, a managed database, a deployment pipeline — without ever filing a ticket. They just apply a manifest, or click through a UI, and the platform provisions it."

"The platform team's job is to build that experience. And to keep building it — improving it, securing it, extending it. Like any product team."
