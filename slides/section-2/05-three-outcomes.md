# Slide 05 — Three Outcomes

**Type:** Summary / payoff — what a good platform delivers  
**Design:** Three-column layout or three strong words: SELF-SERVICE / CONSISTENCY / BOUNDARIES

---

## Key Message

A successful platform delivers three things: self-service for developers, consistency across the organisation, and clear boundaries that make compliance natural.

---

## Talking Points

"So what does success look like? If you build the platform right, you get three things."

"*Self-service.* Developers can provision what they need, when they need it, without waiting for anyone. Namespaces, databases, queues, pipelines — all of it available on demand, within guardrails."

"*Consistency.* Every team's infrastructure looks the same at the platform level, even if their applications are wildly different. Naming conventions, tagging, backup policies, security controls — all built into the golden path. Your auditors will thank you."

"*Clear boundaries.* The platform defines what developers own versus what the platform owns. Developers own their application code and their application-level config. The platform owns the infrastructure, the policies, the standards. No more grey areas about who's responsible for what."

"These three things together solve the cognitive overload problem we talked about earlier. Self-service removes the interruption. Consistency removes the need to re-learn. Clear boundaries remove the ambiguity."

"And this is what we're building on Azure. Using cloud native tools — Kubernetes and the CNCF ecosystem — to deliver these three outcomes in a way that keeps you in control."
