# Slide 05 — Every Team, Every Snowflake

**Type:** Impact statement  
**Design:** Black, "EVERY TEAM. / EVERY / SNOWFLAKE." — middle line "EVERY" in gray

---

## Key Message

Without a platform, every team builds their own way. The result is a unique, fragile, and unmaintainable snowflake for each team — and the platform team inherits all of it.

---

## Talking Points

"Every team figures it out themselves."

"Team A uses GitHub Actions with a YAML they wrote from scratch. Team B is on Azure DevOps with a classic pipeline. Team C copied Team A's YAML but modified it so much it's unrecognisable. Team D has a bash script that nobody touches because nobody understands it."

"Same with infrastructure. Every team has their own Terraform. Or their own Bicep. Or they clicked things in the portal and wrote it down in a doc that's probably out of date."

"Now multiply this by 20 teams. 50 teams. 200 teams."

"Every. Team. A snowflake."

"And when the platform team — or a new engineer — needs to understand how any of this works, they have to rediscover it from scratch. Every time."

"This isn't just a maintenance problem. It's a knowledge problem. It's a trust problem. And as we'll see in a moment — it's a cognitive problem."

---

## Notes

- The rhythm of "Every team" is intentional — use it as a refrain
- The list of teams should feel exhausting to say — that's the point
- The callback to "cognitive problem" seeds the next slide
- Transition: "Because here's what nobody talks about enough — the mental cost of all of this."
