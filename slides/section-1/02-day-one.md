# Slide 02 — Day One

**Type:** Scenario setup, 3-column  
**Design:** White, "DAY ONE." large type, 3 columns: NAMESPACE / DATABASE / PIPELINE

---

## Key Message

When a developer joins a team or starts a new service, they immediately hit walls. The most basic things — getting a namespace, a database, a deployment pipeline — are unclear, undocumented, or require asking someone.

---

## Talking Points

"You join a new team. Or your team starts a new service. Day One."

"You need three things before you can even write a line of production code."

"First: a namespace. Where does this thing live in Kubernetes? Is there a standard? Do I ask someone? Do I just create one? What naming convention? What RBAC? Nobody knows. Or everyone knows differently."

"Second: a database. Do we use Azure SQL? Postgres? Who sets it up? Is there a shared one I join? Do I file a ticket with the DBA team? Is there a Terraform module? Where is it? Is it up to date?"

"Third: a pipeline. Do we use GitHub Actions? Azure DevOps? What's the template? Who has access to the secrets? Who do I ask when it breaks?"

"Three questions. Three different answers depending on who you ask. This is Day One without a platform."

---

## Notes

- Pace slowly through the three columns — let each one build
- Use personal language: "you join", "you need" — make the audience feel it
- The goal is recognition: most people in the room have lived this
- Transition: "So who do you call?"
