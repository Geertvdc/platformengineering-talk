# Slide 01 — The Chaos Before the Platform

**Type:** Section title / impact opener  
**Design:** Black, "THE CHAOS. / BEFORE THE PLATFORM." in massive white type

---

## Key Message

Platform engineering exists because something came before it that wasn't working. That something is chaos — and it's more common than most organisations want to admit.

---

## Talking Points

"Let me start with a word. Chaos."

"Not chaos in the abstract, philosophical sense. Chaos in the very concrete, very practical sense of: nobody really knows how things work, nobody agrees on the right way to do it, and everyone is just trying to get their thing done."

"This is the state most organisations are in before they invest seriously in platform engineering. And the reason I'm starting here is because understanding the problem deeply is the only way to design a platform that actually solves it."

"So — what does this chaos actually look like?"

---
