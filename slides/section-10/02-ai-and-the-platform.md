# Slide 02 — AI and the Platform

**Type:** Forward-looking — AI as a velocity multiplier that demands better platforms  
**Design:** Light slide, heading "AI WRITES THE YAML. THE PLATFORM DECIDES WHAT RUNS."

---

## Key Message

AI-assisted development dramatically increases the rate at which developers generate infrastructure requests. Platforms built for weekly ticket cycles will break under that velocity. The answer is not slower AI — it's better guardrails: policy-enforced, automated, self-service by design.

---

## Talking Points

"One more dimension I want to address before we close: AI."

"GitHub Copilot, Cursor, whatever tool your developers are using — they are getting faster at writing infrastructure code. A developer who used to take a day to write a Terraform module can now do it in an hour. A developer who couldn't write Kubernetes YAML at all can now do it in ten minutes with AI help."

"That's great for velocity. It's a problem for platforms that still operate on ticket queues and manual reviews."

"If your platform requires a human to approve every new database, and developers can now request ten databases a day instead of one — your bottleneck just got ten times worse."

"The platforms we've built today are the answer. Self-service CRs that developers apply directly. Policy gates that run automatically — no human required unless the risk is genuinely high. GitOps audit trails that track every change. All of it scales linearly with developer velocity."

"AI also helps on the platform side. Writing Crossplane Compositions, KRO ResourceGroups, Terranetes policy rules — these are excellent use cases for AI assistance. The patterns are well-structured, the schemas are clear, and AI is very good at this kind of YAML generation."

"The platform team that embraces AI as a tool for building the platform — not just as a risk to manage — will build faster and stay ahead of developer demand."
